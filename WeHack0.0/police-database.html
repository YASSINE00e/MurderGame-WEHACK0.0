📰 RECENT NEWS & BULLETINS

🚨 BREAKING: Second Death at Faculty of Science
[PHOTO: Police at IEEE Office]

HOMICIDE December 14, 2025 — Student Ela Hassine, 21, was found deceased in the IEEE student office. This follows the suspicious death of fellow student Aziz Krifa three days prior. One suspect has been arrested. Both victims were IEEE Student Branch executive committee members.

Case Status: ACTIVE INVESTIGATION — SFX-2025-1214-112

📢 Student Found Dead in Apartment Building
[PHOTO: Residence Building]

SUSPICIOUS DEATH December 11, 2025 — Aziz Krifa, 22, a computer science student and IEEE Student Branch chair, was found deceased at Residence El Ferdaous. Initial reports suggest a fall; police are treating the case as suspicious.

Case reference: SFX-2025-1211-089

🌐 INTERPOL Coordination: International Fraud Ring Operating via Monastir
[PHOTO: Seized Equipment]

INTERNATIONAL December 10, 2025 — Monastir District Police, in coordination with INTERPOL's Financial Crimes Unit, have identified a network of individuals using locally-registered shell companies for cross-border wire fraud. Three suspects are currently at large. Europol has been notified. Case reference: INT-2025-1210-007.

🚔 Drug Seizure at Port of Monastir

DRUG TRAFFICKING December 9, 2025 — Officers seized approximately 14 kg of synthetic narcotics concealed in industrial machinery imports. Two Tunisian nationals and one foreign national of Italian citizenship were detained. The case has been escalated to the National Anti-Narcotics Brigade. Case reference: SFX-2025-1209-099.

📋 Campus Security Enhanced Following Incidents

December 12, 2025 — University administration announced increased security measures across all Faculty of Science buildings. Additional patrols and cameras deployed. Students advised to travel in groups during evening hours.

🔵 INTERPOL Red Notice Received — Human Trafficking Suspect

INTERPOL December 13, 2025 — Monastir District has received an INTERPOL Red Notice for a suspect wanted in connection with a trans-Mediterranean human trafficking network. Local officers have been briefed and are cooperating with Europol and the Italian DIA. Reference: INT-2025-1213-009.

ℹ️ Public Safety Reminder

December 8, 2025 — Police remind students to report any suspicious activity on campus. Emergency contact: 197 | Campus security: +216-XX-XXX-XXX

🔴 RED NOTICE — INTERPOL REF: INT-2025-1213-009
RED NOTICE Human Trafficking — Trans-Mediterranean Network

Subject wanted by Italy (Tribunale di Palermo) and Europol for organizing irregular migration crossings and labour exploitation across North Africa and Southern Europe. Last known location: Sfax region. Monastir authorities on heightened alert. Cooperation agencies: INTERPOL, Europol, Italian DIA, Tunisian National Guard.

Local Contact: Inspector Mehdi Lakhal  |  Filed: December 13, 2025
🔵 BLUE NOTICE — INTERPOL REF: INTB-2025-0841
BLUE NOTICE Identity Verification Request — Financial Suspect

Europol's Economic Crime Unit requests verification of the identity and movements of an individual believed to have transited Monastir between October–November 2025. Subject suspected in connection with a pan-European investment fraud scheme totaling €4.2M. No arrest warrant; information gathering only at this stage.

Local Contact: Detective Leila Mrad  |  Filed: November 28, 2025
🟢 GREEN NOTICE — INTERPOL REF: INTG-2025-0712
GREEN NOTICE Warning — Known Career Criminal in Region

Advisory from Interpol Lyon that a known career criminal with convictions in France, Belgium, and Morocco for serial burglary and identity fraud was released from Moroccan custody October 2025. Subject may be active in the Maghreb coastal region. No specific offences in Tunisia to date; local authorities informed for awareness.

Local Contact: Officer Nabil Ferchichi  |  Filed: October 30, 2025
