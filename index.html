🔍 MURDER INVESTIGATION 🔍

WeHack 0.0 - The Hackathon That Hacked Back

Two IEEE FSM EXCOM members are dead. One person sits in jail. But the truth remains buried in digital evidence, encrypted files, and hidden surveillance footage. Your team must navigate through multiple secure systems to uncover the truth.

💀 The Victims

Aziz Ben Mahmoud - SB Chair, found dead December 11, 2025

Ela Trabelsi - Treasurer, found dead December 14, 2025

⚖️ The Accused

Amine Khelifi - CS Vice Chair, currently detained for Ela's murder. Claims he was framed.

❓ Your Mission

Investigate digital evidence across multiple platforms. Determine who killed whom, who's lying, and who's being framed.

📋 How to Play

  1. Your team will navigate through multiple digital platforms: IEEE Portal, Police Database, Social Media, Security Systems, and more.
  2. Each platform requires access credentials or search parameters. Look for clues in evidence you've already collected.
  3. Not all evidence is available immediately - you must discover clues that unlock new portals and evidence.
  4. Pay close attention to dates, times, usernames, and case numbers - they are your keys to unlocking information.
  5. Some evidence is FORGED. Use forensic analysis and cross-referencing to identify fabricated materials.
  6. Work together as a team. One person operates the computer, but everyone should analyze the evidence.
  7. At the end, your team will present your conclusions about who killed whom and why.
